About Growtech Engineers

Reliable Manufacturing Partner for Industrial Machine Components

Established in September 2023 under the visionary leadership of Mr. Naresh Kumar (bringing over 22 years of hands-on tooling and engineering experience), GROWTECH ENGINEERS has positioned itself as a dependable tooling, machining, and engineering partner for component manufacturers in the Gurgaon–Manesar industrial belt.

We combine proven toolroom craftsmanship with modern CAD-driven toolmaking processes. Our core commitment is helping industrial clients eliminate defect risks, shorten setup cycles, and enhance shop floor productivity through purpose-built tooling.
