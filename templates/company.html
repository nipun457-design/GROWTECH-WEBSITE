About GROWTECH ENGINEERS

GROWTECH ENGINEERS (GTE) was established in September 2023 by Mr. Naresh Kumar at Pachgaon, Manesar (Gurugram, Haryana) — situated directly in Northern India's premier industrial and automotive manufacturing hub along the NH-48 and KMP Expressway corridor.

Backed by over 22 years of hands-on engineering and toolroom experience, our technical team specializes in the design, machining, fabrication, and verification of high-precision jigs, fixtures, receiving gauges, tandem press tools, and progressive hydraulic dies.

With in-house VMC machining, conventional toolroom capacity, dedicated assembly beds, and calibrated inspection equipment, we partner with automotive Tier-1/Tier-2 suppliers and industrial manufacturers to achieve zero-defect repeatability and cycle time reduction.
